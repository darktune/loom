# Studio dress-form asset

`atelier-dress-form.glb` is the Corset model from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets repository.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Corset
- Original credit: © 2017 UX3D; Microsoft (as credited by the source).
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source description: female fabric mannequin with a corset and collar.

Loom normalizes its size, applies approximate proportion scaling, adds a studio stand and lighting, and optionally maps a user-supplied image onto the corset. It is a sample studio dress form, not a reconstruction of the catalog garments or uploaded outfit photos.
