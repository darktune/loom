- Caveman's token-saving claim is not a measured result for this account. Only its communication skill was installed; its separate telemetry-enabled CLI was not installed.
- No databases, crawlers, game engines, secret managers, or OS optimizers were installed. These are cataloged candidates, not agent skills.
- No skill can grant superintelligence, perfect reconstruction, or human work experience. The applied-engineering skill turns that intent into outcome-driven research and verification.
- Rick's supplied YouTube profile was inspected directly. See rick-the-engineer.md for a curated public video index and access limits; full video transcripts and Instagram posts were not reviewed.
